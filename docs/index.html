Field note checked 18 August 2026

An early-game list needs three kinds of answer

A ranked list becomes confusing when it treats every obstacle as the same problem. A player who is short of coins needs a different answer from a player who owns the coins but has not unlocked the route.

I checked one live early-game response using a Members account profile with 250,000 GP, active effort, no Wilderness methods, and a small set of level 20 skills. The response did not force every method into one ranking. It split the results into ready-now, check-before-start, and next-unlock.

Ready means the known gates are clear

The live snapshot put collecting fish food and making pizza bases in the ready group. The first needed no starting cash. The second showed about 27,200 GP of working capital. Those results were useful because the service could say that the supplied stats and cash were enough.

Some requirements still need a person

Filling an empty bullseye lantern appeared under check before start. Its cash estimate was affordable, but the published route assumes a player-owned house in Rimmington. A bank teleport and weight-reducing clothing were also recommended. Hiscores cannot prove any of those details.

Grinding goat horns landed in the same group for a smaller reason. The player needs a pestle and mortar. That tool is reusable, so it does not belong in every action's profit calculation. It still decides whether the player can begin.

The useful distinction is simple. A manual check should stay visible. Silently treating it as passed makes a high-ranked method look more available than it is.

Next unlock should name the gap

The same response put grinding unicorn horns in the next-unlock group because the cash stack was about 49,500 GP short. Cleaning grimy guam leaves had no capital gap, but the supplied profile was two Herblore levels below the requirement. Those are concrete next steps, not generic rejections.

OSRS Money Maker exposes these three buckets on its early-game route. The numbers remain market estimates, and the page warns that quests, access, gear, and items need an in-game check. That warning is part of the recommendation, because account readiness cannot be reduced to one profit number.
