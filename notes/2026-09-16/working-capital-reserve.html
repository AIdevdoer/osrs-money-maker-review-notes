Working capital needs a reserve rule

Putting every coin into the first batch makes a small OSRS account fragile. A slow sale, forgotten travel item, or price move can leave the player unable to continue or switch methods.

The cash input on the early-game OSRS finder should represent what can be spent on the method after a reserve is protected. Decide that reserve first, then enter the usable amount and inspect the ready list.

A conservative first batch may show a lower theoretical return, but it preserves choices. Early progression benefits from liquidity because the player is still discovering which loops, markets, and attention levels actually fit.

The reserve can shrink as confidence grows, but that should be a deliberate decision after completed sales. Profit still sitting in outputs is not available reserve. Count only coins and supplies that can fund the next action without assuming an instant buyer. Liquidity keeps the account able to change course.
