A method card should point beyond its profit number

An OSRS money-making estimate is easier to trust when the player can inspect the underlying method description and requirement source. Without that route, an attractive number becomes difficult to challenge when the account or game state disagrees.

The reviewed examples on the OSRS early-game money-making page include source links and stable requirement details. Use them to confirm the loop, item quantities, and access conditions before treating the live quote as actionable.

Source evidence does not remove the need for a market test. It keeps two questions separate. Is this the right method description, and does the current market make it worth doing?

If the source and the live estimate disagree, pause instead of choosing the more convenient number. Check whether the method definition, recipe, or requirement changed. A transparent disagreement is a reason to investigate, not permission to average incompatible facts. Preserve the source date when recording the correction. That date tells the next reader what still needs checking.
